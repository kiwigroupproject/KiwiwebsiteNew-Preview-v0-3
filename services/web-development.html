FAQ

Questions about Websites

How long does a website take?

A business website usually takes 3 to 5 weeks. Stores and platforms take longer depending on scope.

Will a redesign hurt our Google ranking?

Not when it is planned. We map every existing URL, set up redirects and monitor Search Console through the migration.

Can we edit the content ourselves?

Yes. We set up a simple editor so your team can update text, images, projects and posts.
