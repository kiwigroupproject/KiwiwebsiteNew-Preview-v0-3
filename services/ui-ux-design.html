FAQ

Questions about UI

Do you design only, or build too?

Both. You can take the designs to your own developers, or we can build them.

How do you validate designs?

We test prototypes with a handful of real users before development, which catches most issues cheaply.

Do you follow accessibility standards?

Yes. We design for contrast, keyboard use and readable type, aiming at WCAG AA.
