Food & beverage rebrand

The challenge

Where things stood

A growing food brand had outgrown its first logo. Packaging varied by product, shelf presence was weak next to larger competitors, and the story behind the brand was lost.

Our approach

What we did

We ran a discovery workshop, defined the brand personality and built a distinctive identity with a flexible colour system.

A packaging architecture gave all 30 products a shared look with clear range and flavour cues.

A full brand guideline and templates kept printers, agencies and the in-house team consistent.
