Who We Are

Logic and art, under one roof.

Most firms are strong at engineering or at design. We built KiWi to close that gap, so the product your customers use and the brand they remember are shaped by one team with one standard.

FAQ

Questions we hear often

How long does a typical website or app take?

A business website usually takes 3 to 5 weeks. Custom software projects depend on scope; we give a fixed timeline once discovery is complete.

Will I own the source code and design files?

Yes. Source code, design files and credentials are handed over in full on final payment.

Do you work with clients outside India?

Yes. We work remotely with weekly demos, a shared project board and overlapping working hours.

Do you offer support after launch?

We offer monthly plans covering updates, hosting, backups, monitoring and small enhancements.

Can you redesign our existing website without losing search ranking?

Yes. We map every existing URL, set up redirects and monitor Search Console through the migration.
