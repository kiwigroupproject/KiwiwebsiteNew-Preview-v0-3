Who we are

Our story

Built to close the gap between code and craft

Most companies are strong at engineering or at design. Few are good at both, and the handoff between two vendors is where quality gets lost.

KiWi Technologies was founded to remove that handoff. Engineers and designers sit in the same team, share the same brief and answer to the same standard, so the product your customers use and the brand they remember feel like one thing.

Today we work with founders and leadership teams across logistics, retail, food, education and services, building software, platforms and identities that are made to last.
